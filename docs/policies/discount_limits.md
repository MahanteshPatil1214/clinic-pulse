# Discount Limits by Tier

Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2026-03-01

## Tier Definitions
Tier A covers clinics with an annual order value above 100000 synthetic credits. Tier B covers 40000 to 100000 credits. Tier C covers clinics below 40000 credits. Tiers are reviewed once each quarter using synthetic data only.

## Maximum Discounts
Discount limits by tier are listed here for each synthetic clinic tier.
Tier A may receive up to 8 percent on a single approved order. Tier B may receive up to 12 percent. Tier C may receive up to 15 percent. These ceilings apply to the discount itself and not to bundled shipping or training offers.

## Approval Rules
Any discount above 5 percent requires regional manager approval. Any discount at or above the tier ceiling requires an additional finance review. The representative must record the business reason in the request. The system never applies a discount automatically.

## Expiry
Approved discounts are valid for 30 days from the approval date. Unused approvals expire silently and must be requested again if still needed.

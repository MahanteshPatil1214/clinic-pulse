# Targeted Promotions Policy

Version: 1.3
Effective date: 2026-01-15

## Purpose
This synthetic policy describes how sales representatives may propose targeted promotions to veterinary clinics. All examples and figures are fictional and for demonstration only. A human manager approves every promotion before it is offered.

## Eligibility
A clinic may be considered when its three-month order value is at least 15 percent below its prior three-month value, or when visit counts fall for two consecutive months. Clinics with an open credit hold are not eligible. New clinics in their first six months are not eligible.

## Allowed Offers
This section lists what a representative can offer a clinic with falling orders.
Representatives may propose a one-time bundled starter kit, a free training webinar for clinic staff, or a volume commitment review. Offers must be framed as suggestions for the clinic to consider, never as an automatic adjustment.

## Request Process
Submit a promotion request with the clinic identifier, the observed trend, the proposed offer and the policy section that supports it. The regional manager reviews and decides within five business days. No discount is applied by any automated system.

## Exclusions
Promotions are not available for controlled substances, for clinics with a disputed invoice, or for products already listed on the seasonal clearance list.

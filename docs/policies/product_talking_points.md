# Product Talking Points

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2025-11-10

## Vaccines
Vaccines support preventive care and help clinics plan seasonal demand. When order value declines, review the clinic's vaccine share change and ask whether their immunization schedule has moved. Do not promise clinical outcomes.

## Diagnostics
Diagnostics products support in-clinic testing workflows. A falling diagnostics share may indicate a change in supplier or testing volume. Ask open questions before suggesting a product review.

## Nutrition
Nutrition lines are often the most predictable recurring revenue. A sharp nutrition share change can signal a dosing or purchasing change. Offer a product education session where permitted by policy.

## Compliance
Representatives must use only approved synthetic product claims. Never state or imply that any product treats, prevents or cures a disease unless the approved label says so. Escalate medical questions to the clinic's veterinary professional.
